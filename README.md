# Piggy Bank Toy 🐷

A tiny interactive visual toy about saving money. Drop coins into the piggy bank, but the bank can only hold 20. Add the 20th coin and a hammer swings in and smashes it. Then you can restart and fill it up again.

**Live demo:** [your live link]

## The idea
Saving money is usually abstract, just a number going up. I wanted to make it physical and a little mischievous. Every coin you add builds anticipation, because you know the hammer is coming. The payoff is the break, and the restart makes you want to play again.

## How it works
- Each interaction adds one coin to the piggy bank
- The bank has a limit of **20 coins**
- When the 20th coin lands, a hammer animation plays and breaks the bank
- A restart button resets the toy to an empty piggy bank
